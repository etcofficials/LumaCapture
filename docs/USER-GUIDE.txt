LumaCapture 2.0.0 - user guide
==============================

STARTING
  Installer version: Start Menu > LumaCapture (or the Desktop shortcut).
  Portable version: double-click LumaCapture.exe in the LumaCapture folder.
  Settings, the library list, logs and crash reports are kept in the
  "LumaCapture-data" folder next to the program when that folder is writable,
  otherwise in %LOCALAPPDATA%\LumaCapture. Recordings go to your
  Videos\LumaCapture folder unless you choose another one (Output).

THE RECORD PAGE
  Left    Capture Mode - Display, Window, Region or Game (Beta) and what to
          record; Sources - webcam, microphone, system audio on/off; Output -
          folder, file name, container; Quick Settings - profile, resolution,
          frame rate, quality.
  Centre  Live preview of exactly what will be recorded, the timer, the Record /
          Pause / Stop / Screenshot buttons, audio meters, and your recent
          recordings.
  Right   Video, Audio, Webcam, Effects and Overlays settings.

RECORDING
  1. Choose a capture mode and target.
  2. Press Record (Ctrl+Alt+R). LumaCapture's own windows are not recorded.
  3. Pause/Resume with Ctrl+Alt+P; Stop with Stop or Ctrl+Alt+R.
  4. "Saving..." is shown until the file is finished and checked; then it
     appears under Recent Recordings and in the Library.
  Game (Beta) records the whole monitor the chosen game window is on - this
  works for full-screen and borderless games. Window capture records one window
  even when covered; on Windows 10 Windows draws a yellow border around it.

RECOMMENDED SETTINGS FOR OLDER QUAD-CORE PCs (e.g. i5-2400S, no hardware encoder)
  Source size (1080p) at 30 FPS, Balanced quality - or 1280x720 at 60 FPS.
  The Video tab shows an estimate (Light / Moderate / Heavy / Very heavy) and a
  resolution guide for this PC. If a recording falls behind (drops frames)
  LumaCapture says so; lower the resolution, frame rate or quality.

QUALITY PRESETS (x264 on the CPU)
  Low = smallest files, Balanced = recommended, High / Very High = sharper and
  larger files with slightly more CPU. Advanced (Video tab) shows CRF, x264
  preset, keyframe interval and threads.

WEBCAM (Webcam tab)
  Choose the camera and mode; the preview runs while the tab is open.
  Auto adjust analyses the picture once and sets brightness, contrast and
  colour; Preset and Reset; Before / after; picture sliders.
  Camera & lighting shows only the controls your camera supports:
    Anti-flicker - 50 Hz (Europe, Asia, Africa, Australia) or 60 Hz (Americas)
      if you see rolling bands under room lights.
    Low light - "Smooth motion" keeps the full frame rate (darker);
      "Brighter image" lets the camera expose longer (blur/stutter in dim rooms).
  If the stats line says frames are held, the room is too dark for the sensor:
  add light facing you, choose "Smooth motion" and use Auto adjust. Software
  cannot add detail the sensor never captured.
  Placement, size, opacity, shape and border are in the Overlays tab.

OVERLAYS (Overlays tab)
  Webcam position/size/opacity/shape, text and image overlays, watermark.
  "Open layout editor" lets you drag everything on a picture of the frame.

LIBRARY
  All recordings with thumbnail, duration, resolution, frame rate, size and
  date. Search, sort, Play, Open with, Show in folder, Rename, Details (the
  file's real properties) and Delete (to the Recycle Bin). Import adds existing
  videos; you can also drag video files onto the window.

HOTKEYS (Settings > Hotkeys)
  Ctrl+Alt+R start/stop   Ctrl+Alt+S stop   Ctrl+Alt+P pause/resume
  Ctrl+Alt+M mute mic     Ctrl+Alt+X screenshot
  They work while other programs have focus and must use Ctrl/Alt/Win (or an
  F-key) so they never interfere with typing.

SCREENSHOTS
  Screenshot (Ctrl+Alt+X) saves a PNG of the selected display, window or region
  to the screenshots folder (Settings > Output). LumaCapture's windows are not
  included.

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - Errors appear in a banner at the top with what to do next.
  - After a crash or power loss LumaCapture offers to recover the recording
    ("...-recovered.mkv"); at most about one second is lost.
  - Logs and crash reports: Settings > Advanced. Nothing is ever uploaded.
  - Feedback: Help / About (Instagram @etcofficials, etcofficials28@gmail.com,
    or a GitHub issue).

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - Video is encoded on the CPU; 1080p at 60 FPS is experimental on older CPUs.
  - Window capture on Windows 10 shows a yellow border drawn by Windows.
  - Region capture must lie inside one monitor.
  - No echo cancellation: use headphones with a microphone.
  - Background removal only with a green screen (no AI segmentation).
