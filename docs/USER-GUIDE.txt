LumaCapture 1.0.1 - user guide and manual test checklist
=========================================================

STARTING
  Double-click "Launch-LumaCapture.bat" (or LumaCapture.exe) in the
  LumaCapture folder. It is portable: nothing is installed, and the folder can
  be moved anywhere (paths with spaces are fine). "Create-Desktop-Shortcut.bat"
  adds a desktop shortcut after asking you first.

  Settings, history, logs and crash dumps are kept in the "LumaCapture-data"
  folder next to the exe. Recordings go to G:\video (change this with
  "Change..." or in Settings > General).

BASIC WORKFLOW
  1. Source: Display / Window / Region.
  2. Video: resolution, frame rate, quality and encoder speed.
  3. Audio: system audio and/or microphone (meters, volume, mute).
  4. Webcam: switch "Show webcam in recording" on. "Image & camera..." adjusts
     the picture; "Layout & overlays..." places it.
  5. Press "Start recording" (or Ctrl+Alt+R). Stop with the same button or
     hotkey.
  6. The panel shows the state: Recording / Paused / Saving. "Saved" appears
     only after the file has been finalised and checked. The recording is then
     listed under "Recent recordings" (Play, Show in folder, Delete).

RECOMMENDED SETTINGS FOR THIS PC (i5-2400S, GT 730 - no hardware encoder)
  - Native (1080p) 30 FPS, encoder "ultrafast", Balanced quality.
  - 720p 60 FPS "ultrafast" also kept up in tests. 1080p60, and slower presets
    at 1080p, dropped frames under continuous motion (the CPU throttles under
    load).
  - Watch the "Dropped" tile while recording. If it rises, lower the frame
    rate or resolution.

WEBCAM QUALITY
  Open "Image & camera..." (Settings > Webcam & filters). The camera runs while
  this page is open.
  - Auto adjust: analyses the current picture once and sets brightness
    (gamma/exposure), contrast and white balance; in dim scenes it adds light
    noise reduction. It does not keep changing afterwards, so the picture does
    not "pump". Press it again if the lighting changes.
  - Preset...: Natural, Brighter, Warm, Cool, Monochrome, Low light, as
    starting points. Reset returns to the unprocessed image.
  - Before / after: the original and the processed picture side by side.
  - Filters on: switches all processing on or off.
  - The Image tab has manual sliders: brightness, contrast, gamma, exposure,
    saturation, temperature/tint, RGB, sharpen and denoise.
  - Camera & lighting tab (the camera's own controls; only those it supports
    are shown):
      Anti-flicker - set it to your mains frequency (50 Hz in most of Europe,
        Asia and Africa; 60 Hz in the Americas) if you see rolling bands
        under room lights.
      Low light - "Smooth motion" keeps the full frame rate (darker picture);
        "Brighter image" lets the camera expose longer (the picture is
        brighter, but motion blurs and stutters in dim rooms).
      Exposure, gain, white balance, focus, backlight and others each have an
        Auto box and a Default button.
  - The line under the camera settings shows the real frame rate and the
    longest gap between frames. If it reports that the camera is holding
    frames, the room is too dark for the sensor at that frame rate. Add light
    facing you, or choose "Smooth motion" and use Auto adjust.

  Why does the webcam seem to "shutter"?
    Measured on a Logitech C270 in a dim room: the camera exposed for 62 ms
    (longer than one 30 fps frame), so it held frames and motion looked jerky.
    This is a limit of the sensor, not flicker from the lights and not a
    LumaCapture slowdown. More light, "Smooth motion" and Auto adjust give the
    best result.

DEFAULT HOTKEYS (change them in Settings > Hotkeys)
  Ctrl+Alt+R start/stop   Ctrl+Alt+S stop   Ctrl+Alt+P pause/resume
  Ctrl+Alt+M mute mic     Ctrl+Alt+X screenshot

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
  - Errors appear in a banner in the recording panel, with the next step to
    take.
  - A crash or an interrupted recording: the recording is written
    crash-safely (at most about the last second is lost). At the next start,
    LumaCapture offers to recover it as "...-recovered.mkv". The original file
    is kept.
  - Logs: LumaCapture-data\logs. Crash and hang dumps:
    LumaCapture-data\crashdumps. Nothing is ever uploaded.

KNOWN LIMITATIONS
  - Window capture on Windows 10 shows a yellow border drawn by Windows.
  - A minimised window keeps showing its last image. A closed window stays on
    its last image until you stop (choose it again afterwards).
  - Region capture must lie inside one monitor.
  - No echo cancellation: use headphones with a microphone.
  - Background blur/removal only works with a green screen (chroma key); there
    is no AI segmentation.
  - Screenshots capture the source only (no webcam or overlays burned in).
  - MP4 output is created by converting the finished MKV (MKV is safer).

MANUAL TEST CHECKLIST
  Recording (screen)
    [ ] Display source, 1080p30: record 30 s with a moving window, then stop.
        The file plays in a media player and lasts about 30 s.
    [ ] Pause for about 5 s in the middle, resume, then stop. The video skips
        the pause and the audio stays in sync (clap or speak on camera around
        the pause).
    [ ] Press Start twice quickly, and Stop twice quickly: nothing breaks, and
        exactly one recording is saved.
    [ ] Window source: choose a window, record, move/resize it, minimise and
        restore it. The recording follows the window.
    [ ] Region source: "Select region...", drag, Enter; edit X/Y/W/H; record.
    [ ] MP4 container (Settings > General): after stopping, an .mp4 appears
        (and the .mkv too if "Keep the MKV" is ticked).
  Audio
    [ ] Play music with "System audio" on and record 20 s: the music is in the
        file.
    [ ] Microphone: the meter moves when you speak. Mute (Ctrl+Alt+M) silences
        only the mic.
    [ ] Settings > Audio "separate tracks": the MKV has 3 audio tracks.
  Webcam
    [ ] Turn the webcam on and off several times in the main window: the
        window never freezes.
    [ ] Open "Image & camera...", press Auto adjust, compare with Before /
        after, try each preset, then Reset.
    [ ] Camera & lighting: switch Anti-flicker between 50 and 60 Hz under room
        lights, and try both Low light options.
    [ ] Layout & overlays: drag/resize the webcam and try circle, rounded,
        border, mirror and crop. Record and check that it matches the preview.
    [ ] Unplug the camera while recording: the screen recording continues.
        Plug it back in: the camera recovers within a few seconds.
  Recovery and errors
    [ ] Start a recording and end LumaCapture with Task Manager. Restart it:
        it offers to recover, and the "-recovered.mkv" plays.
    [ ] History: Delete... asks for confirmation and moves the file to the
        Recycle Bin.
